Quick Tips

Study tips you can use today

🧠

Use active recall

Close your notes and try to explain what you remember. This turns studying into retrieval practice instead of simply rereading.

🔁

Space your studying

Instead of doing everything in one session, review important material across several days.

📱

Remove distractions

Put unnecessary notifications and distracting websites out of reach during your study session.

🗣️

Teach the topic

Explain the concept as if you were teaching someone else. Gaps in your explanation reveal what you need to review.

✍️

Practice, don't just read

Use practice questions, problems, flashcards, and exercises to check whether you can actually use the material.

😴

Protect your sleep

Avoid turning every study session into an all-nighter. Regular sleep is an important part of learning and memory.

💡 Random Study Tip

Start with the hardest task while your attention is fresh.
